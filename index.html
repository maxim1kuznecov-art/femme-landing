Your stylists

People who understand — because they've been there.

Every stylist on our team specializes in gender-affirming, feminizing makeup — not as one of many services, but as their primary focus. And every one of them has a personal connection to this work: they identify within the trans and non-binary community, or have spent years working closely with it.

That means you won't need to explain your situation, your pronouns, or why you're there. Your stylist already gets it.

What our stylists specialize in: contouring that softens without masculinizing, eye makeup for hooded and asymmetrical eyes, foundation for mature and oily skin, beard coverage that doesn't cake, liner that actually stays.

Most of our clients are 45 and older — and our stylists are trained specifically for that. Mature skin needs a different approach: lighter layers, careful product placement, techniques that work with your skin rather than against it. If you've ever felt like makeup advice online was made for someone thirty years younger — this is the opposite of that.

Every session is one-on-one. Your stylist takes notes, reviews your products, and sends a personal guide after your sessions. The goal isn't to teach you everything at once — it's to give you one clear step forward that you can actually use tomorrow morning.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to buy new products before the session?
No. Bring what you have and we'll start there. Your stylist will show you how to get the most out of your current collection before suggesting anything new.
Is this confidential? Will anyone see the recording?
Sessions are private and used only for your stylist's notes — to help prepare your guide and remember your specific situation. Nothing is shared with anyone, ever.
I'm still semi-closeted. Is this safe for me?
Yes. This is remote — you can join from wherever you feel comfortable. You don't need to use your real name. We are not affiliated with any organizations that would have access to your information.
I already have makeup experience. Is this still useful?
Absolutely. Most clients have some experience — the issue isn't knowing nothing, it's having no reliable system. Your stylist works with where you are, not from scratch.
What if I want more sessions after the first two?
Many clients come back when they're ready for the next area — eyes after foundation, lips after eyes. You book additional sessions whenever you want. No packages you have to commit to upfront.
What do I need for the session?
A phone or computer with a camera, decent lighting, and the makeup products you currently use. Your stylist will work with what's in front of you.
I'm in a conservative state / area. Does that matter?
That's exactly what this service is for. Everything is remote. Your stylist will also send product links so you can order everything online — no store visits required.
I'm over 50. Is this really for me?
Yes — you're actually our typical client. Most of the people we work with are 45–65, many of whom started their journey later in life. Our stylists specialize in mature skin and understand the specific challenges: texture, fine lines, products that behave differently than they do in tutorials filmed on 20-year-olds.
Can I reschedule?
Yes, anytime up to 24 hours before the session — no fees, no questions.
